About

I'm a Cybersecurity-focused Computer Science student at the Egyptian Chinese University, currently completing the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) Cybersecurity Track. Most of my work sits at the intersection of two things: finding how a system fails, and building the tooling that notices when someone else tries the same thing.

That shows up as penetration-testing labs on one side — recon, exploitation, reporting — and behavioral anomaly detection on the other, through my graduation thesis on insider-threat detection. I like problems that force me to think like both the attacker and the analyst reading the alert.

CASE-01 / thesis

CyberDNA — Insider Threat Detection System

Graduation thesis modeling insider threats through behavioral fingerprinting: an event pipeline, autoencoder-based detection engine, and a risk-scoring layer feeding an explainable, real-time dashboard — trained against the CERT insider-threat dataset.

Python/FlaskPostgreSQLPyTorchWazuh
CASE-02 / design

BioVault — Biometric-First Privileged Access

A PAM concept where employees authenticate with biometrics but never see the underlying credentials — the vault handles checkout, session monitoring, and audit logging. Grounded in NIST SP 800-63 and WebAuthn/FIDO2, shaped by the Verkada breach.

PAM designWebAuthn/FIDO2NIST 800-63
CASE-03 / lab

SQL Injection & SSH Hardening Labs

Exploited UNION-based SQL injection and enumerated an Oracle database using Burp Suite and SQLmap. Ran an SSH brute-force attack with Metasploit, then hardened the target with Fail2ban and sshd_config changes.

Burp SuiteSQLmapMetasploit

CTF & Cryptanalysis
